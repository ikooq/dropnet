# Label-blinded review of 30 new ranges

One reviewer rated 30 cases. According to the user, their background is data analysis, risk assessment and suspicious telephone activity; they did not participate in DropNet development, did not see the selection key or prior answers, did not use AI for evaluation, and consented to anonymous publication of the results and experience description. These are user-reported conditions; professional credentials and identity have not been independently verified. The response filename alone is not evidence of AI use. Cards showed factual counts, so labels were blinded but evidence was not masked.

Confidence ratings: 12 at 3/5, 13 at 4/5 and 5 at 5/5. One reviewer judged usefulness as an investigative lead; these are not verified fraud labels. Deliberately balanced strata are not a population sample and cannot establish precision, recall, common control or wrongdoing.

Control C04 received a positive investigation recommendation for three DEBT complaints involving three numbers, filed October 28–30. The counts and dates were checked against the underlying evidence table. This suggests a possible lead, not a proven false negative. C23 and C24 had no subject, and their clarity was rated no. Several threshold cases were uncertain due to broad categories or mixed subjects. Requested information included narratives, call timestamps and verified origin; availability in public source data is not established.

The sample excludes the 60 earlier reviewed blocks and 12 performance-study blocks but uses the same FTC snapshot. It is neither a new temporal dataset nor independently verified fraud ground truth. A separate timed comparison remains unperformed. The full case selection and answers are kept outside this repository while the study is underway.
