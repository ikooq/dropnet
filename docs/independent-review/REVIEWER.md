# Reviewer instructions

You are reviewing evidence summaries based on public FTC Do Not Call complaints. The complaints and caller IDs are unverified; they do not establish fraud, spoofing, number ownership, or that callers share an operator. Do not contact number owners or recommend blocking solely from these cards.

For each case in `cases.md`, independently record one row in `answers.csv`:

- `reviewer_id`: an agreed anonymous identifier, consistent across rows.
- `evidence_clear_yes_no_unsure`: `yes`, `no`, or `unsure` — is the evidence presented clearly enough to understand what was observed?
- `investigate_yes_no_unsure`: `yes`, `no`, or `unsure` — does the evidence justify **further investigation**, given its limitations? This is not a verdict of wrongdoing.
- `confidence_1_to_5`: 1 = low, 5 = high confidence in your assessment.
- `missing_information`: what would you need to assess the case better?
- `reason`: a short reason for your assessment, including concerns or alternative explanations.
- `reviewer_expertise`: a brief description of relevant experience; repeat it or provide it separately to the coordinator.

Complete every case if possible. Use `unsure` where evidence is insufficient; leave a field empty only if you cannot answer. Do not consult the selection key or discuss individual cases with the project team before completing the review. The numbers shown are a limited, sorted excerpt of reported caller IDs; the counts cover the full range of reports in the snapshot. Filing dates are not necessarily call dates. Similar prefixes or consumer area codes do not prove common control. Return your completed copy to the coordinator and agree on what may be published.
